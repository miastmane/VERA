02 / Marketing strategy

The 4Ps of VÉRA.

VÉRA uses the marketing mix to connect its fashion offer with the needs and shopping habits of its target customers.

01 — PRODUCT

Style for every day

Contemporary clothing and accessories that balance trend-led design with wardrobe versatility.

  • Women’s and men’s collections
  • Everyday basics and statement pieces
  • Shoes, bags and accessories
02 — PRICE

Accessible fashion

A range of prices to attract customers looking for modern style without premium-designer costs.

  • Accessible wardrobe basics
  • Higher-priced statement items
  • Clear prices and seasonal offers
03 — PLACE

Online and in store

Convenient shopping channels help customers discover and buy collections on their own terms.

  • Responsive online store
  • Physical shops and pop-ups
  • Flexible collection and delivery
04 — PROMOTION

Inspire the look

Visual storytelling and digital content help customers imagine how pieces fit into their lives.

  • Fashion photography and lookbooks
  • Social media and creator content
  • Email updates and launch news
